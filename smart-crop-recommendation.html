About this recommendation engine

Mannvasam scores each crop by comparing your entered nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, pH, temperature, humidity, rainfall and soil type against known agronomic ranges for that crop, then blends the results into a single suitability score out of 100.

This is a guidance tool, not a replacement for a certified soil-testing lab or your local agricultural extension officer.
